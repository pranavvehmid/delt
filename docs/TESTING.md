# Testing DELT with your own inbox

Thanks for helping. This takes about 10 minutes to set up, then a few days of normal use.

## 1. Install

- **Android:** download [DELT.apk](https://github.com/pranavvehmid/delt/releases/latest/download/DELT.apk) and open it.
- **iPhone or laptop:** open [delt-mu.vercel.app](https://delt-mu.vercel.app).

Sign in with your `@vitstudent.ac.in` account. DELT reads and labels mail; it can't delete anything.

## 2. Use it for a few days

Open it when you'd normally check mail. While you do:

- **A mail in the wrong pile?** Open it and tap **Wrong pile? Report it**. You'll get a GitHub issue with only the sender's domain and the pile filled in (never the subject or names). Say which pile it belongs in, describe the mail in general words, add your batch year, and post. Issues are public.
- **A deadline missing or wrong?** Same button. Say what the date should have been.
- **Something broken or confusing?** Account menu → Settings → **Report a problem**.

Optional:
- Turn on **Notifications** in Settings and tell us if you get too many, too few, or late ones.
- Try both looks (◐ → Minimal / Glyph) and the themes.

## 3. Tell us how it went

Comment on [issue #1](https://github.com/pranavvehmid/delt/issues/1) with:

- your batch year and branch (for example `2025 BEC`)
- your phone and browser
- roughly how many mails were in the wrong pile out of how many
- anything you'd change

Please don't paste mail bodies, register numbers or anyone's contact details.
